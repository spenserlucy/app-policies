Accessibility review

Clipsway 0.1.2 (version code 26), closed-test edition.

Public playback and download. No account or sign-in is required.

Download the Accessibility review MP4

Duration: 231 seconds (3 minutes 51 seconds). H.264 MP4, 1080 x 2400, 30 fps, 7.2 MiB. The recording is silent.

Demonstrated flow

  • Open Clipsway and withdraw its in-app toolbar consent; the toolbar shows Not enabled.
  • Open the disclosure, use Cancel, see Not enabled, then open the disclosure again.
  • Read all six paragraphs of the in-app Accessibility disclosure in the captured app UI.
  • Tap Agree, open Android Accessibility settings, use Allow in the system prompt and verify the service is On.
  • Open the toolbar History beside an external app's empty editor, paste saved record A and use Select all.

Recording context

The video combines real recordings from separate takes at normal speed. Cuts remove idle waits; it is not presented as an uninterrupted first-install session.

Section cards announce changes between takes. These are explanatory editorial cards, not captured app UI or a substitute for the in-app disclosure. The captured disclosure is not artificially frozen or extended.

The History opening and paste/Select all demonstrations are separate takes; the latter starts with History already open. The consent withdrawal shown is inside Clipsway, not a demonstration of Android Accessibility service revocation.
