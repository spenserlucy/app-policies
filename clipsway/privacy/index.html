Closed-test edition

Builds visibly marked as the closed-test edition provide Pro free to every user of that build. No purchase, subscription or trial activation is required. This access belongs to the test build; it is not a permanent purchase and does not grant Pro in a later standard edition.

The closed-test edition does not initialize Google Play Billing or read/write local trial and purchase-receipt storage. Previously stored trial and purchase information is left unchanged. The trial, purchase and automatic Billing-diagnostic behavior described below applies to standard builds, not to this closed-test mode. Network permissions and bundled libraries may still be present; this is not a claim that the APK has no network permissions.

All local-history handling, Shizuku and Accessibility disclosures, separate consent, notification requirements and Stop controls remain unchanged. Free Pro does not enable recording or the toolbar, or grant system permissions.

Local information and purpose

Clipsway is a local clipboard history and text-input utility. It stores text you save, optional titles, creation/use times, favorites and categories. A content digest, search copy and link indicator support matching and search.

You may create records, share selected text to Clipsway or import a backup through the system file picker. It does not automatically read another app's private storage.

Local settings include language, appearance, history capacity, feature settings, recording choices and a boot-count checkpoint for recovery, toolbar consent/rules and tutorial progress. Toolbar rules include application package names; up to 64 application-specific positions may be remembered.

Standard edition: trial and Google Play purchases

The app is free to download. A locally managed 7-day Pro trial starts on the first app launch for this installation. It does not automatically charge you or become a subscription. Trial timing, the last observed device time and boot/elapsed-time checkpoints are stored privately on the device. Clearing app data or reinstalling can reset this local trial; it is not an account-based, first-ever-download eligibility service.

Pro is a non-consumable, one-time purchase through Google Play. The app receives signed purchase information, including the product/package identifiers, purchase token, purchase time and purchase state. It verifies and stores the signed receipt locally to restore access across app launches, and asks Google Play to confirm delivery. It does not send the receipt, your history or your settings to a developer-operated server. Temporary connection failures do not by themselves cancel a saved, verified purchase. A later successful Google Play ownership check may remove access after a purchase is revoked.

Google Play handles checkout, its account and payment information, purchase/restore requests and transaction processing under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms. Clipsway does not receive your payment-card number or Google account password. Google may make transaction and financial reports available to the developer through Play Console for order support, accounting and legal obligations; this does not expose your clipboard history.

The Google Play Billing library and its supporting components use network access for payment-related services and automatic billing diagnostics. Billing initialization, connection and ownership/product checks can generate diagnostic events even if you do not make a purchase. These events can include the app/package and SDK versions, Android version, device manufacturer/model and operating-system build information, memory size, language/country settings, network type and mobile-network operator codes, event times, a randomly generated billing-client instance correlation value, and billing-operation/error results. The build fingerprint describes the operating-system build; it is not a claim that the app reads a unique hardware identifier or GPS location. Google processes this technical information to operate, secure and diagnose its billing services. Clipsway has no in-app switch to disable this SDK logging separately. It does not supply clipboard history, editor text or toolbar display rules to these components for billing or diagnostics. This network access means the app is not a network-permission-free application.

After the trial ends, free history, manual text operations, import/export and optional automatic recording remain available. Trial expiry or purchase revocation does not delete records. Buying or restoring Pro does not grant Android or Shizuku permissions, accept a disclosure, or automatically enable the toolbar.

Optional Shizuku recording

Recording is off on first use. You must separately install/start Shizuku, authorize Clipsway, then explicitly start recording after reading its in-app disclosure. Permission alone does not start first-time recording.

A privileged Shizuku service monitors subsequent system clipboard changes for eligible single ordinary-text items. No text field, keyboard or toolbar needs to be visible. It does not read clipboard content already present before that recording session.

Sensitive-marked content, images, files, URI/intent and multiple-item clips, blank/oversized text and Clipsway's own clipboard writes are skipped. Sensitive markings depend on Android and the source app and cannot identify every password or verification code. Stop recording before copying private material.

Recording is global: toolbar allow/block lists do not limit it. It pauses while locked and does not recover missed content. A visible ongoing notification and the Connection page provide Stop.

Clipsway remembers your explicit recording choice and may restore it after an app update. After you accept the recording disclosure that includes restart recovery, a phone restart may also restore your saved enabled choice if you have not cancelled it with Stop. Choices saved before this behavior was introduced require one confirmation of the updated disclosure; you do not need to stop an active session first or confirm after each restart. Restart recovery is considered after normal boot completion, usually after the first unlock, not during locked boot.

Recovery still requires Shizuku, existing authorization and visible notifications. Clipsway does not start Shizuku or grant permission for you. Android background-start restrictions may prevent immediate recovery; opening Clipsway can retry pending recovery. Ordinary app launches, normal disconnections, revoked permission and process exits within the same boot do not newly enable recording when no recovery is pending. Recovery does not read existing clipboard content or recover missed text. Stop clears the saved enabled choice and pending recovery, not existing records.

Optional Accessibility toolbar

The toolbar requires a separate disclosure and explicit consent. It is an input utility, not an accessibility tool primarily designed for people with disabilities.

It accesses the current application package, window/keyboard positions and editor state/position to show the toolbar and verify actions you tap. Text-change events temporarily hide the toolbar; their text is not collected or saved. Select all checks the current text length. On Android 13+, the current input session/connection validates the target and requests user-tapped paste, select all, undo or redo.

It does not monitor/read the system clipboard or automatically save text-field content. It displays ordinary records already saved locally; password-category records are excluded. Pasting writes your chosen record to the Android clipboard and requests paste in the destination editor.

The toolbar/history list do not take input focus. Only the separate saved-record editor you open takes focus; it edits that saved record, not another app's text, and attempts to return focus after save/cancel.

It hides for detected password fields and a locked screen. Incorrectly marked password fields cannot be reliably identified. Disabling/withdrawing the toolbar does not stop Shizuku recording. Withdrawing removes remembered positions but retains history and display rules. Manual history works without Accessibility, and manual saving works without Shizuku.

An app update may require renewed consent when the disclosure changes. Until you confirm, toolbar access is suspended even if your previous enabled choice is remembered. Remembering that choice is not new consent.

Storage and security limits

Clipsway has no developer-operated history upload, cloud synchronization, advertising, or developer analytics/crash-reporting service. Clipboard history and app settings are stored locally and are not automatically transmitted to a developer server. Google Play purchase and billing-diagnostic processing is described separately above.

Records are kept in app-private local storage, without additional database encryption or app-level authentication. The password category separates display/query results; it is not an encrypted password vault or a replacement for a dedicated password manager.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled by the app's configuration. The app does not claim protection against a compromised device, privileged access or copies you export.

Sharing, exports and other apps

Sharing sends text to the app you select. Exporting sends a backup to the storage provider/location you select. Those recipients may upload/process data under their own policies.

JSON backups are not encrypted and include password-category records, if any. Their checksum checks integrity, not confidentiality. Choose a trusted destination and delete unwanted external copies yourself.

Pasting places text on the Android clipboard and supplies it to the chosen editor, subject to Android and the destination app. Policy and tutorial links open in a browser, which may access the network. Clipsway cannot control or delete copies held by recipients.

Diagnostics

Toolbar diagnostics hold up to 40 recent entries in memory concerning package names, geometry and service/decision state, without clipboard or editor text. A report is copied only when you choose the diagnostic copy action. Any subsequent sharing is your choice.

Retention and deletion

Unfavorited ordinary history defaults to 200 records, adjustable from 10 to 500. Capacity trimming applies to eligible ordinary records, not favorites/password-category records. Imports may preserve extra records rather than silently discard them.

Delete records individually as needed. Clear ordinary history deletes only unfavorited ordinary records, retains favorites/password-category records and does not clear the Android clipboard.

Clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes local app data under Android's controls. This does not delete external backups, shared copies or the system clipboard; manage those separately.

There is no Clipsway account or developer-hosted account database. Local trial and receipt data are removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling, but Google Play's transaction records are governed by Google's retention rules. You can restore a valid Pro purchase through the same Google Play account after reinstalling. Deleting local app data is not a refund request and does not remove Google Play's purchase history.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling also does not delete billing diagnostics already sent to Google. Clipsway does not control those server-side records, cannot delete them from the app, and has not established a specific retention period for this SDK's diagnostics. Google's processing, available privacy controls and applicable deletion rights are described in the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Contact messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so we can answer your request. Do not send passwords or private clipboard content. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete correspondence by emailing the privacy contact.

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it in a browser, GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page adds no advertising, analytics, cookies or external fonts. Opening the website does not give it access to your Clipsway history.

Changes and contact

The published policy shows its effective date. Sensitive-access changes requiring renewed consent will be disclosed in the app before that access is enabled.

本地处理的信息与用途

随贴是一款本地剪贴板历史与文字输入工具。它保存您选择保存的正文、可选标题、创建与使用时间、收藏和分类。内容摘要、搜索副本和链接标记用于去重、检索与展示。

您可以新建记录、将选中文字分享给随贴，或通过系统文件选择器导入备份。应用不会自动读取其他应用的私有存储。

本地设置包括语言、外观、历史容量、功能设置、自动收录选择与恢复所需的开机次数检查点、浮条同意与规则、教程进度。浮条规则包含应用包名；最多可记住 64 个应用的专属位置。

可选的 Shizuku 自动收录

首次使用时，自动收录默认关闭。您需要另行安装并启动 Shizuku、授权随贴，在阅读应用内披露后明确开启收录。仅授予权限不会开始首次收录。

具有特权的 Shizuku 服务监听之后发生的系统剪贴板变化，只处理符合规则的单条普通文字。此时不要求文字输入框、键盘或浮条可见。它不会读取本次收录会话开始之前已存在的剪贴板内容。

应用跳过带敏感标记的内容、图片、文件、URI/Intent、多条目剪贴板、空白或过长文字，以及随贴自身写入的剪贴板内容。敏感标记依赖 Android 和来源应用，无法识别全部密码或验证码。复制私密信息前请停止收录。

收录作用于系统剪贴板，浮条的应用允许或屏蔽名单不限制收录范围。锁屏期间暂停，不补录遗漏内容。持续可见的通知和连接页面均提供停止入口。

随贴记住您明确选择的收录状态，可在应用更新后尝试恢复。您同意包含重启恢复的收录说明后，手机重启也可按条件恢复已保存且未点击停止取消的开启选择。此前保存的选择需确认一次更新后的说明；不必先停止正在运行的收录，也无需每次重启都确认。重启恢复仅在普通开机完成后处理，通常在首次解锁后，不在解锁前启动。

恢复仍需 Shizuku、既有授权及可见通知就绪，随贴不替您启动 Shizuku 或授予权限。Android 后台启动限制可能阻止立即恢复，此时可打开随贴重试待恢复操作。没有待恢复操作时，普通启动、正常断开、权限撤回及同一次开机内的进程退出不会新增自动开启。恢复不读取当时已有的剪贴板，也不补录空档中的文字。停止收录会清除开启选择与待恢复状态，不会删除已有记录。

可选的无障碍浮条

浮条需要单独披露并取得明确同意。它是输入工具，不是主要面向残障人士设计的无障碍辅助工具。

浮条访问当前应用包名、窗口与键盘位置、编辑器状态与位置，用于显示浮条并核验您点击操作的目标。文字变化事件仅用于暂时隐藏浮条，不收集或保存事件中的文字。全选会检查当前文字长度。Android 13 及以上版本使用当前输入会话与连接核验目标，执行您点击的粘贴、全选、撤销或恢复。

浮条不监听或读取系统剪贴板，也不会自动保存输入框内容。它显示已在本地保存的普通记录，不显示密码分类记录。粘贴时，会将您选择的记录写入 Android 剪贴板，并请求在目标编辑器粘贴。

浮条和历史列表不获取输入焦点。只有您主动打开的独立记录编辑器获取焦点；它编辑的是已保存记录，而不是其他应用中的文字，保存或取消后尝试归还焦点。

检测到密码框或锁屏时隐藏浮条。对于未正确标记的密码框，应用无法可靠识别。关闭或撤回浮条授权不会停止 Shizuku 收录。撤回同意会移除记忆位置，但保留历史记录和显示规则。不启用无障碍仍可手动查看历史，不使用 Shizuku 仍可手动保存。

当披露内容变化时，应用更新可能要求重新确认。在您确认之前，即使记住了此前的开启选择，浮条访问也会暂停。记住开启选择不等于新的同意。

存储与安全限制

随贴没有开发者运营的历史上传、云同步、广告或开发者分析/崩溃上报服务。剪贴板历史和应用设置保存在本地，不会自动传送到开发者服务器。Google Play 购买及支付诊断处理已在前文单独说明。

记录保存在应用私有的本地存储中，没有额外的数据库加密或应用级身份验证。密码分类只隔离展示和查询，不是加密密码保险箱，也不能替代专用密码管理器。

应用配置禁用了 Android 自动云备份与设备迁移。不承诺防御已被攻破的设备、特权访问或您导出的副本。

分享、导出与其他应用

分享会将文字交给您选择的应用；导出会将备份交给您选择的存储提供商或位置。接收方可能按照自身政策上传或处理数据。

JSON 备份未经加密，如有密码分类记录，也会包含在内。校验和只检查完整性，不提供保密性。请使用可信目的地，并自行删除不再需要的外部副本。

粘贴会把文字放到 Android 剪贴板，并交给所选编辑器，受 Android 和目标应用的规则约束。政策和教程链接由浏览器打开，可能访问网络。随贴无法控制或删除接收方持有的副本。

诊断

浮条诊断在内存中最多保留 40 条近期包名、几何位置和服务决策状态，不含剪贴板或编辑器正文。只有您主动点击诊断复制操作时，报告才会被复制。之后是否分享由您决定。

保留与删除

未收藏的普通历史默认保留 200 条，可调整为 10 至 500 条。容量裁剪只针对符合条件的普通记录，不针对收藏或密码分类记录。导入时可能保留超过上限的记录，而不是静默丢弃。

您可以逐条删除记录。清空普通历史只删除未收藏的普通记录，保留收藏与密码分类记录，也不会清空 Android 系统剪贴板。

在 Android 设置中清除应用存储或卸载应用，会由 Android 删除应用本地数据。这不会删除外部备份、已分享副本或系统剪贴板，请分别管理。

应用没有随贴账号或开发者托管的账号数据库。清除应用存储或卸载会移除本地试用与回执数据，但 Google Play 交易记录由 Google 的保留规则管理。重新安装后，可以通过同一个 Google Play 账号恢复有效的 Pro 购买。删除本地数据不等于申请退款，也不会清除 Google Play 的购买历史。

清除应用存储或卸载也不会删除已发送给 Google 的支付诊断。随贴不控制这些服务器记录，无法从应用内删除，也尚未确认此 SDK 诊断的具体保留期限。Google 的处理方式、可用隐私控制和适用的删除权利，请参阅前文链接的 Google 隐私政策。
